Common Questions

Everything You Might Want to Know

Do I need to book ahead for the Tuesday or Thursday Slimming World groups?

No appointment is required — you are welcome to come along to any session time at Maldron Hotel Tallaght (Tuesdays at 9:30am, 4:00pm, or 6:00pm) or Divine Mercy SNS, Lucan (Thursdays at 6:00pm). If you feel nervous walking in alone, text or call Pamela on 086 123 1774 beforehand and she'll look out for you.

How are Online 1-on-1 Coaching sessions different from Slimming World group?

Slimming World groups focus on weekly community support and Food Optimising. Online 1-on-1 Life & Weight Coaching gives you dedicated, private time with Pamela via video call to work deeply on mindset, emotional eating, self-belief, stress habits, and personal goals — tailored 100% around your life.

Can I do Online 1-on-1 Coaching even if I don't live near Tallaght or Lucan?

Yes! All 1-on-1 coaching sessions are held online, so you can work with Pamela from anywhere in Ireland or abroad.

Is GLP-1 support available in group?

Yes, Pamela's groups provide warm, practical support for members using GLP-1 medication alongside healthy eating and sustainable habit-building. Message Pamela directly if you'd like a confidential chat before attending.
